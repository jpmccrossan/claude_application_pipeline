# Find job listings with web search

Using web search (at most 15 searches), find job listings posted in the last two weeks that match the
applicant's PREFERENCES: first on the careers pages of the companies listed under companies to check,
then on public job boards for their locations.

Only include listings with a direct URL to one specific job (not a search page) and text in a language
the applicant accepts. Never include anything from sites that ask search engines not to index them
or that require a login.

Reply with only this, nothing else (empty list if nothing found):
<listings>
[{"company": "...", "title": "...", "url": "https://...", "location": "...", "deadline": "YYYY-MM-DD or empty"}]
</listings>
